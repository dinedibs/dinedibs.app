Delete your Dine Dibs account

Last updated November 15, 2026

You can delete your Dine Dibs account yourself in the app, or ask us to do it by email. Deleting is permanent.

In the app

1. Open Dine Dibs and go to Profile.

2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.

3. Tap Delete my account to confirm.

The account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Restaurant accounts: tap the account button at the top left of the dashboard, then Delete account. If you own a restaurant that is live on Dine Dibs or waiting for approval, email us first so it can be handed to another owner or taken down.

Without the app

Email help@dinedibs.app from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Your sign-in and profile (name, email address, profile picture), your saved deals and restaurants, the restaurants you hid, your punch cards and any free meals you earned, the codes you hold that were not used, your notifications and the notification token for your phone, and the approximate location we saved to show deals near you.

What is kept

Records of codes that staff scanned (purchases, stamps and free meals) are kept for the restaurant’s accounting, with your identity removed: they no longer point to you. A report you sent us about a deal is kept without your name. If you owned a restaurant, its listing keeps the business contact details you gave for it until the listing itself is removed. Our backups keep account data for up to 90 days after deletion and are then overwritten.

More about what we collect is in the Privacy Policy.
