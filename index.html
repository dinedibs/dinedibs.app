Punch cards

Every visit gets you closer to a free meal.

Every visit earns a punch, with a deal or without one. Fill the card and your next item there is free.

  • Deal or no deal, every visit earns a punch. Just show your code.
  • Each restaurant picks how many punches fill the card (3 to 10) and what the free item is worth.
  • A free meal you've earned keeps its value, even if the card changes later.
