What Dine Dibs is

Dine Dibs shows you limited-time deals from local restaurants and lets you claim one, walk in, and show a QR code. The restaurant honors the deal at the counter. You never pay Dine Dibs anything.

Restaurants pay Dine Dibs a small fee only when a diner gets one of their deals. Views and claims are free for everyone.

Your account

You can browse deals without an account. To claim a deal you sign in with Apple, Google or your email. Keep your sign-in method secure; you are responsible for activity on your account.

You must be 13 or older to use Dine Dibs. If you are under 18, use it with a parent or guardian.

Claims and QR codes

A claim gives you a single-use QR code. Show it to staff at the restaurant named on the code (during the deal’s hours, if it has set hours). Each code works once and is good for 7 days, even if the deal ends sooner. You can hold up to 3 unused codes at a time.

Deals come from the restaurant, not from Dine Dibs. The restaurant sets the terms, availability, and rules, and may pause or end a deal at any time. If a restaurant does not honor a claimed deal, tell us and we will follow up with them.

Do not share, sell, copy, or attempt to reuse QR codes. Doing so may lead to your account being closed.

Punch cards and free meals

Restaurants that offer a punch card give you one punch each time staff scan your code there: a claimed deal, or a stamp code you get in the app after a purchase without a deal (one stamp per restaurant per day). Filling a restaurant’s punch card unlocks a free meal at that restaurant: one item up to the amount the restaurant sets. Each restaurant sets how many punches fill its card and how much the free meal can cost, and shows both on the card. The free meal is used as its own visit and not combined with a deal. Punches and free meals stay with the restaurant where you earned them, have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and may change or end with notice in the app. A restaurant may set unfinished cards to reset after 3, 6 or 12 months without a visit; the card shows this, and we remind you a week before. A free meal you already earned keeps the amount it had when you earned it, and stays good at that restaurant even if it stops offering a punch card.

Acceptable use

Use Dine Dibs the way a good neighbor would. Do not abuse claims, create fake accounts, scrape the service, interfere with restaurants, or use the app to break the law.

Restaurant accounts

If you list a restaurant, you confirm you are authorized to act for it, that your deals are accurate, and that you will honor claimed deals during the posted window. You are billed monthly, at the rate shown in the app, each time a diner gets one of your deals and staff scan it. Views, claims, free meals, and visits where the diner did not get the deal are never billed.

Restaurants must follow the Restaurant rules in this app, including the rules on photos and the ban on alcohol deals. We may pause deals or listings that break them.

Restaurant rules

Every restaurant on Dine Dibs agrees to these when it signs up.

Deals

Post deals you will honor, during the days and hours you choose. Keep the title, price and rules accurate. Pause a deal if you can’t honor it.

Say "2 for 1", not "BOGO". Don’t post deals that require a purchase you don’t mention, and don’t change the terms at the counter.

No alcohol

Dine Dibs deals are for food and non-alcoholic drinks only. Don’t post deals on beer, wine, cocktails or any other alcohol, including happy hour specials. California law bans giving alcohol away, and Dine Dibs doesn’t promote alcohol at all. Deals that mention alcohol are blocked automatically.

Photos

Only upload photos you took, own, or have permission to use. Don’t copy photos from Google, Yelp, delivery apps or other websites.

Don’t upload photos where customers or staff can be recognized unless they have agreed to it.

Photos should show your real food and your real place. No text-heavy flyers, logos of other brands, or misleading images.

Reports and enforcement

Diners can report a deal that isn’t honored, is misleading, or uses someone else’s photo. We review every report, may contact you, and may pause a deal or listing while we sort it out.

Questions about these rules: help@dinedibs.app.
