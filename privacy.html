Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

The short version: we collect what the app needs to show you deals near you and keep your punch cards, we never sell it, and restaurants only ever see your first name and last initial.

What we collect

Account details you give us: your name, email and, when you sign in with Google, your profile picture, and which sign-in provider you used. Restaurant owners also give us their name, a phone number, a contact email and the restaurant’s address; an owner who invites staff gives us the email address of each person invited.

If you ask to join the waitlist from outside our launch area, we keep the email address and city you enter so we can tell you when we open there. Email us to have them removed.

Location, only while you use the app, to show deals near you. We also save an approximate location (rounded to about 1 km). If you turn on new deal alerts, we use it to tell you about new deals nearby, at most once a day. We never track location in the background and never share it with restaurants.

Activity in the app: deals you claim and save, restaurants you hide, reports you send us, and your verified purchases. This powers your punch cards and free meals.

Device information for push notifications, if you allow them. We use it for reminders about your own codes, punch cards and free meals, and for new deal alerts only if you turn those on.

What restaurants see

When staff scan your QR code, the restaurant sees your first name and last initial, the deal, and whether you have visited before. They do not see your email, phone number, location history, or visits to other restaurants.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show you third-party ads. We do not store payment details for diners, because diners never pay through Dine Dibs.

Who we share with

Service providers that run our infrastructure: Supabase (hosting and sign-in), Expo (push notifications), Resend (sign-in emails), Sentry (crash reports) and, for restaurants, Stripe (statements and payments, which receives the restaurant’s name and contact email) and Anthropic (each photo a restaurant uploads is checked automatically before it is shown; nothing about diners is sent). Crash reports and a small sample of speed measurements include your device model, app version and what went wrong, tied to an anonymous account ID, never your name, email, phone or location. Maps and address lookups come from Apple on iPhone and Google on Android. These providers process data for us.

Authorities when the law requires it.

Your choices

New deal alerts are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off again in Profile. You can hide a restaurant from any of its deals and show it again in Profile. Turn location and notifications on or off in your device settings at any time. Sign out or delete your account from Profile. When you delete your account we remove your profile, saved deals, punch cards, free meals and the codes you had not used. Records of codes that were scanned are kept for the restaurant’s accounting with your name and account removed, and a report you sent us stays without your name.

California residents can ask what personal information we hold and request deletion by emailing help@dinedibs.app.

Security and retention

Data is stored with access controls so each person only sees their own records. QR codes contain no personal data. We keep your account data while your account is active and for up to 90 days after deletion for backups. Records of scans and of changes to deals and restaurants are kept for 18 months.
